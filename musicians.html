The problem

Three things
holding you back

You're good at what you do. The music industry infrastructure isn't built to help you.

Pain 01

No single profile for bookings

Your EPK is a PDF from 2022. Your Instagram is full of personal content. Your website costs €15/month and three clicks to find. Venues want one link, one page, one fast answer to "who are you."

Pain 02

Fans can't follow your gigs

Spotify is for streams. Instagram is for clips. Bandcamp is for merch. Nowhere lists "every gig this person is playing" — so fans never know where to look.

Pain 03

Venues find you the hard way

Bookers stumble onto you through other bands, word of mouth, late-night Instagram scrolls. No easy way for a venue to find a sax player or a punk trio when they need one.
