The problem

Every event.
The same
three problems.

Before the night, during it, and after. Evntry fixes all three.

Before the event

You never know who's actually coming

Paper lists, WhatsApp reservations, inbox messages — spread across three places. Someone says they're coming. Do they mean it? You find out at the door. A paid ticket is a real commitment. An "interested" click isn't.

On the night

The door is always chaos

Cash box, handwritten list, someone's phone with a spreadsheet. The first 30 minutes of every event is a queue and an argument. Slow entry kills the vibe before the first song even starts.

After the event

You have nothing to show for it

You had a great night. 150 people loved it. Then it's over — no names, no emails, no data. Next week you're back to zero, promoting to the void, hoping the same crowd sees your post.

Features

Everything
you need.
Nothing you don't.

Stripped down to what actually matters for a local venue.

Zero fan fees

The price you set is exactly what fans pay. No booking fee at checkout. More fans complete the purchase — less drop-off at the last step.

Scan the door from your phone

A dedicated fullscreen check-in view for door staff. Scan QR codes, see live attendance count, get instant pass/fail feedback. No printer, no clipboard.

Your permanent venue page

Every venue gets a shareable link — put it in your Instagram bio or Linktree. Fans see all your upcoming events in one place, year-round.

Live sales + attendance

See tickets sold in real time before the night. Then watch the check-in counter climb as people arrive. Plan staff and bar stock with actual numbers.

You own your attendee data

Every buyer's name, email, and ticket status is yours — exportable as CSV anytime. No platform lock-in, no asking permission to reach your own audience.

Free, paid, or pay at door

Free entry, fully ticketed, or reserve-now-pay-at-door. All three modes work. You choose per event — no extra setup for each.

Questions

Common
questions.

Do fans pay extra fees?
No. The price you set is exactly what fans pay at checkout. Evntry never adds booking fees on the fan side — no sticker shock, no drop-off at the last step.
Does Evntry hold my money?
Never. Every payment goes through your own connected Stripe account and is paid straight into your bank. Evntry never touches ticket revenue.
Is there a contract?
No. No long-term contract and no lock-in. Pro is month-to-month, and you can export your attendee data anytime.
What happens after I sign up?
A real person reviews every new venue account, usually within a day. We'll help you set up your first event so it's ready before you share the link.
How quickly do I get paid?
Via your connected Stripe account. Payouts follow Stripe's schedule — typically 2–7 business days after each sale. No waiting for a monthly settlement from a platform.
What happens after my first event?
Your first published event is free, whether it uses free or paid tickets. Before publishing another event, contact us about Pro and we'll tailor pricing to your venue and volume.
Do I own my attendee data?
Completely. Your attendee list — names, emails, purchase history — belongs to you and is exportable anytime. We never share it or use it for our own marketing.
Do I need a credit card to get started?
No. You can start and create drafts without a card. A connected Stripe payout account is required for paid tickets, and standard Stripe processing fees apply.
