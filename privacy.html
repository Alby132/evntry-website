Privacy
Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Evntry ("we", "us", "our") operates the evntry.app website and mobile application. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used and protected. We comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

Evntry is operated by Alberto Solinas, based in Croatia. You can contact us at hello@evntry.app.

2. Data we collect

Venue account data: When you create a venue account we collect your name, email address, and the information you provide about your venue.

Event and ticket data: When you create events or fans purchase tickets, we store event details, ticket transactions, and buyer information (name, email, ticket QR code) on your behalf.

Payment data: Payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers or sensitive payment data. We receive a transaction record confirming the payment.

Usage data: With your consent we use Google Analytics to collect anonymous data about how visitors use our site (pages visited, session duration, device type). This data does not personally identify you.

Cookies: We use a single first-party cookie to remember your cookie consent choice. Analytics cookies are only set with your explicit consent.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide and operate the Evntry service
  • To process ticket sales and send email confirmations to buyers
  • To allow venues to manage attendee lists and door scanning
  • To send service-related communications (account updates, support replies)
  • To improve the platform using anonymised analytics (with consent)

4. Your attendee data

Buyer information collected during ticket purchase (name, email, ticket status) belongs to the venue organizer. Evntry acts as a data processor on behalf of the organizer. We do not use attendee data for our own marketing, and we do not sell or share it with third parties except as required to operate the service (e.g. sending ticket confirmation emails).

5. Legal basis

We process data under the following legal bases: Contract performance (to deliver the ticketing service), Legitimate interests (fraud prevention, service security), and Consent (analytics cookies).

6. Data retention

Account data is retained for as long as your account is active plus 90 days after deletion. Transaction records are retained for 7 years to meet financial record-keeping obligations. You can request earlier deletion where legally permitted.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time (does not affect processing already done)

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@evntry.app. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Third-party services

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage analytics (consent only). Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — hosting and CDN. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

9. Security

We use HTTPS for all data in transit. Access to production systems is restricted to authorised personnel. We implement industry-standard security practices and review them regularly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new "last updated" date. For material changes, we will notify venue account holders by email.

11. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email hello@evntry.app.
