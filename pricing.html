Questions

The obvious
answers.

Do fans pay any extra fees?
No. The price you set is exactly what fans pay at checkout. Evntry never adds booking fees on the fan side. That's one of the reasons fans actually complete the purchase — no sticker shock at the end.
Do I own my attendee data?
Yes, completely. Your attendee list — names, emails, purchase history — belongs to you. You can export it anytime. We never share it with third parties or use it for our own marketing.
How do I get paid?
Directly through Stripe. When a fan buys a ticket, the revenue goes to your connected Stripe account — minus the Evntry fee (3% on the free plan, lower on custom) and Stripe's standard card processing fee (~1.4% + €0.25 for European cards). Payouts follow Stripe's normal schedule — typically 2–7 business days.
Can I sell free tickets?
Yes. Free events have no fees at all — no 3%, nothing. You can also mix free and paid tiers on the same event (e.g. free general entry, paid VIP table). Free ticket tiers are always free regardless of your plan.
What if I only do a few events a year?
Stay on the free plan. You pay 3% only when tickets actually sell — no monthly cost, no risk. Start free and reach out when you're ready to scale.
How is the custom plan priced?
It depends on how often you run events, how many tickets you sell, and what features you need. We work through it together — book a call and we'll put together a number that makes sense for your venue. No generic tiers, no surprises.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Custom plans have no lock-in unless you've agreed to a fixed contract. Your events and attendee data always stay accessible.
Is there a Stripe fee on top of the Evntry fee?
Yes — Stripe charges their own payment processing fee separately (typically ~1.4% + €0.25 for European cards, higher for non-EU cards). This goes directly to Stripe, not to us. On the free plan the only fees are 3% (Evntry) + Stripe's processing fee. Custom plans can reduce or eliminate the Evntry portion.
Is Evntry available in my country?
Evntry is rolling out across Europe. Stripe payments work in all major European markets. If you're outside the EU, email us at hello@evntry.app and we'll let you know the timeline.
